Announcing a new resource Mendocino Coast residents can use to
discover potential grid-scale solar development sites. I created this
site to provide analysis to the Mendocino Planning Department that
could help enable Alternative Energy Facilities, Offsite (AEF:O)
overlays in Coastal Rural Residential (RR) zones.

The [Mendocino Coast Solar](https://jmacd.github.io/mendo-coast-solar/)
site identifies over 70 candidate parcels in or near the Mendocino
coastal zone that could accommodate a grid-scale solar facility. We
learned that five Coastal RR-zoned parcels would become eligible under
the adoption of a Local Coastal Program amendment granting this civic
use.

Caspar Water Company is pursuing a grid-scale solar development on its
parcel south of Fern Creek Road as a means to improve its financial
stability. This site was the location of a 1920s residential
development known as the "Knob Hill" of Caspar. We view it as
essential that Caspar maintains its current relatively dense zoning
plan, and we believe a solar development today will enable future
residential development on this site.
