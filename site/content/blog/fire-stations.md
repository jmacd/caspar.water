Today I learned that [Fort Bragg
Fire](https://fortbraggfire.specialdistrict.org/) is a special
district, a Joint Powers Authority between the City of Fort Bragg and
the surrounding rural fire district which covers Caspar through
Inglenook. We had a meeting in Caspar with the Fort Bragg Fire chief
who explained how home fire insurance rates are heavily influenced by
having a fire station within 5 road-miles distance, and that Caspar
would be a good site at the south end of the district. Even an
unmanned station, if properly equipped, can qualify nearby homeowners
for better insurance rates.

The group looked at a photo of the Mendocino Volunteer Fire Department
station at 42800 Caspar Little Lake Road. This is one such unmanned
station, 3 miles from the Caspar Community Center. I wondered how many
homes in Caspar are not already within 5 miles of an existing station.
A fire station in Caspar sounds nice, but let's have some numbers.

How many homes would be covered that were not already?  I put together
a data set using a [Medical Emergency Response Structures data
set](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=2c36dbb008844081b017da6fd3d0d28b)
maintained by the U.S. Geological Survey, which lists active fire
stations nation-wide. Combined with a [Mendocino County
Parcels](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=95fc4d1c8ede4b21b3dd8fa89a75d39b#overview)
data set and road network information from
[OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/), this [website
displays the impact of a hypothetical new fire station in Mendocino
County](https://jmacd.github.io/mendo-coast-fire/).

Then, something interesting. There is no fire station at 42800 Caspar
Little Lake Road in the data set! Without that station on the map,
there are 69 existing homes in parts of Caspar that are not within 5
miles of a fire station. With that station, and there is only one
house covered by a new station in Caspar. (To be continued.)
