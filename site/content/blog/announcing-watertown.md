[Watertown](https://github.com/jmacd/watertown) is software for water
systems by Caspar Water Company.

The California Surface Water Treatment Rule applies to public drinking
water systems that treat and serve surface water or groundwater under
the direct influence of surface water. This regulation's **15-Minute
Rule** requires monitoring systems to record disinfectant levels
around the clock.

On top of that, monitoring and recording must be physically located on
site. Off-site and cloud-based monitoring systems do not meet these
requirements. Public water systems with surface water sources are
required to have on-site observability system, ready to display
historical disinfectant levels with at least 15-minutes of resolution.

Watertown is free software for water systems with a built-in
timeseries database. It runs on low-power computer hardware, publishes
data to a local web browser, and integrates with cloud-based storage
and alerting services.

Watertown is modern, open-source software, a SCADA system with
out-of-the-box OpenTelemetry, MQTT, and Modbus. Interested in
Watertown?  Get in touch.
