[Watertown](https://github.com/jmacd/watertown) is software for water
systems by Caspar Water Company.

The California Surface Water Treatment Rule for public drinking water
treatment systems are legally required to show their water is safe
around the click. The **15-Minute Rule** requires treatment systems to
record disinfectant levels around the clock.

On top of that, monitoring and recording must be physically located on
site. Off-site and cloud-based monitoring systems do not meet these
requirements. Practically speaking, public water systems with surface
water sources are required to have on-site observability system, ready
to display historical disinfectant levels with at least 15-minute
resolution.

Water system operators, are you paying for commercial software to meet
this requirement? Watertown is free software for water systems with a
built-in timeseries database. It runs on low-power computer hardware,
publishes data to a local web browser, and integrates with cloud-based
storage and alerting services. 

Watertown is modern, open-source software, a SCADA system with
out-of-the-box OpenTelemetry, MQTT, and Modbus. Interested in
Watertown?  Get in touch.
