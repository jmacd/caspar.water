The pH-adjustment system recirculates a small stream of water from the
2-inch main through a venturi and back to the storage tank. The venturi draws
in air, and an eductor disperses that air as bubbles in the tank.

{{ figure src="/img/ph-adjustment.svg" caption="pH adjustment recirculation: a variable-speed pump returns water from the treatment-plant main to the top of the tank, where entrained air is released through an eductor." /}}

Air bubbles promote carbon-dioxide off-gassing, shifting the carbonate
equilibrium toward fewer hydrogen ions and a higher pH:

<div class="science">
<strong>CO₂ + H₂O ⇌ H₂CO₃ ⇌ H⁺ + HCO₃⁻</strong><br>
<strong>O₂ + HCO⁻ ⇌ HCO₃⁻</strong>
</div>
